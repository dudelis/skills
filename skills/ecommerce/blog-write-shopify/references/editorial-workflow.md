# Editorial planning and bilingual writing

Read when creating an article or substantially changing its angle or promotions.
Small draft corrections use the update workflow without repeating the full
planning interview.

## Gather the material and selected resources

Accept a working name/topic, pasted text in any language, distributor articles,
source URLs, supplied images, and product/collection links in any combination.
Reuse information already supplied; ask only for what is still missing.

Ask which products or collections the user wants to promote inside the article.
Resolve product URLs to actual Shopify catalog entries. External distributor
links provide source facts; do not invent corresponding storefront URLs.
Present ambiguous catalog matches for the user's selection.

Separately ask for the existing collection to place below the article. The body
may feature products outside that collection. Do not change collection
membership to align these two selections. Prepare the collection-section
heading in German and English as separate structural values.

Offer additional relevant catalog products with a reason for each suggestion.
Prefer products with real customer-facing pages and suitable purchase
availability. The user makes the final decision; research or relevance does
not authorize adding a suggested product to the finished article.

Ask for a banner file or image URL. If the user will generate it separately,
ask what exactly they want depicted before preparing the generation prompt.
Clarify style, composition, product-image references, and any requested text
only as needed. Derive image dimensions from the target theme and image guidance.
Prepare this prompt early so article preparation can continue while the user
creates the banner. Do not generate the image yourself. New article creation
waits for the supplied image; updates reuse the existing banner unless changed.

## Inspect the store and plan the SEO approach

Discover the connected store, locales, existing blogs, and relevant articles.
Inspect two or three examples for German and English writing, HTML, excerpts,
author attribution, image placement, buttons, metafields, and template usage.
Recommend an appropriate existing destination blog and layout in the brief.
Do not invent an expert author or credentials.

Analyze the intended reader, concrete problem, search intent, likely search
phrasing, related questions, and overlap with existing YuliSkin content. A
request containing only a topic should receive useful angle ideas and a
recommendation before the finished article is written.

Select primary and supporting keyword themes separately for German and English.
Use public search research to understand questions and intent where helpful;
do not fabricate search volume, difficulty metrics, or ranking guarantees.
Research current reliable sources for factual claims, especially skincare and
health explanations. Manufacturer/distributor documentation supports product
facts; scientific and public-health sources support health explanations.

For supplied material, extract verified facts and create an original angle,
structure, examples, and wording for YuliSkin. Avoid copying distinctive phrasing
or paraphrasing each source sentence in order. Preserve official product names,
accurate facts, and any attribution required by the supplied material. Do not
promise that the source can never be recognized.

Record source provenance and unresolved factual gaps in the local brief.
Distributor materials are background sources and are not automatically linked
in the customer-facing article. If external evidence links would help, propose
them in the brief for approval; they are separate from promoted products.

Discover relevant internal destinations: approved products, collections,
existing articles, and useful pages. Plan descriptive anchors and natural
placement. Verify real locale-specific URLs instead of guessing translated
handles. Do not modify other articles to create links to the new post.

Choose article length according to the reader's problem and completeness of the
answer. Do not set a supposed Google-required word count, pad the article,
stuff keywords, or add irrelevant FAQs. Use current authoritative SEO guidance.
[Google content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content),
[Google link guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/links-crawlable)

## Present the planning review

Present a concrete brief containing:

- Recommended angle, intended reader, problem to solve, and factual gaps.
- German/English keyword focus, outline, and proposed length with its reason.
- Destination blog, author attribution, and suitable existing HTML layout.
- Requested body promotions and additional product suggestions with reasons.
- The separate associated collection and German/English section headings.
- Proposed internal links and any proposed external evidence links.
- Supplied-banner status or the requested generation prompt.

Wait for approval of the approach and final product/collection selections before
writing the complete article. Combine these decisions into one review when
practical, and respect selections already explicitly made by the user.
This planning approval authorizes local preparation only. Shopify inspection
remains read-only until the separate confirmation of the completed creation.

## Produce the article and separate fields

Write precise, useful German with "du" and natural English. Keep both versions
factually equivalent and include the same approved promotions, while adapting
wording and keyword phrasing to each language. Avoid unsupported clinical claims,
invented studies, or expert credentials.

Follow the topic-appropriate store layout. Product-led sections can use the
existing alternating image/text rows and buttons; explanatory sections can use
clear paragraphs and headings. Scope embedded CSS to the article body and make
rows stack sensibly on mobile. Write HTML fragments; the theme supplies the
article's main title and surrounding document.

Include real links and suitable verified images for promoted body products and
collections. Prefer authentic Shopify catalog images. If a promoted collection
has no image, ask for imagery or approval to use a representative product image.
Do not invent packaging or add unselected product features. Write descriptive
ALT text in each HTML body's language.

Use contextual HTML links with descriptive anchors, not arbitrary link or
keyword counts. Check localized destinations. Keep SEO metadata and collection
headings outside the body HTML as separate fields.

Prepare titles, HTML summaries/excerpts, SEO titles/descriptions, and section
headings in German and English. Count final SEO strings, including spaces, and
shorten naturally to the stated limits rather than truncating sentences.

Create a descriptive, lowercase ASCII German handle with hyphens. Transliterate
handles/filesystem names only: ä to ae, ö to oe, ü to ue, and ß to ss. Preserve
German spelling in prose and metadata. Provide a localized English handle if
supported by the live translation keys. Keep existing handles on updates unless
a change is requested and confirmed.

Store the approved brief, sources, two HTML bodies, and field manifest locally.
Include all titles, excerpts, SEO, handles, resource selections, section headings,
and the supplied/downloaded banner and inline image assets. Save asset copies
under `assets/`, preserving user originals. Keep pending uploads and existing
Shopify CDN URLs mapped in the manifest; local HTML previews may use local assets.
Save a banner prompt only when requested.

Present links to the completed local files and a creation preview identifying
the target store/blog, both language versions, metadata, associated collection,
section headings, banner, and images that need uploading. Ask explicitly whether
to create this unpublished Shopify draft and upload those images. Wait for the
answer; elapsed time, planning approval, or a local manifest is not confirmation.
Local revisions can continue before this confirmation without Shopify writes.

The final creation confirmation covers that reviewed payload, its necessary
image uploads, metadata, and translations. After approval, substituting the
mapped local image references with Shopify CDN URLs is part of that payload.
Substantive changes after the preview require renewed confirmation. Record
uploaded media/article IDs as soon as they exist; later article revisions need
a separately confirmed update preview.
